Q1. Which Indian port recorded the highest container throughput in the fiscal year 2022‑23?

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust (JNPT)
  2. Mundra Port
  3. Chennai Port
  4. Visakhapatnam Port

  1. JNPT
  2. Dahej

  4. Kandla

  1. Bhilai Steel Plant

Q4. The proposed Vadhavan Port in Maharashtra is envisaged as which type of facility?

  1. Container transshipment hub
  2. Bulk cargo terminal

Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (a) Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust (JNPT) - JNPT handled over 5.5 million TEUs in FY2022‑23, the highest in the country.

Q2. Answer: (b) Dahej - Dahej in Gujarat hosts India’s first west‑coast LNG regasification terminal commissioned in 2004.

Q3. Answer: (c) Visakhapatnam Steel Plant - RINL operates the Visakhapatnam Steel Plant in Andhra Pradesh.

Q4. Answer: (d) All‑weather deep‑draft port - Vadhavan is planned as a deep‑draft, all‑weather port to accommodate large vessels.

Q5. Answer: (a) Delhi‑Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) - Dholera Special Investment Region is a flagship node of the DMIC.

Q6. Answer: (b) Salt - Kandla is India’s leading port for salt exports, handling the bulk of the country’s salt shipments.

Q7. Answer: (c) Tamil Nadu - The Sriperumbudur EMC, a major electronics hub, is situated in Tamil Nadu near Chennai.

Q8. Answer: (d) All of the above - NICDC oversees the development of all designated industrial corridors in India.

Q9. Answer: (a) Maharashtra - Maharashtra leads with the highest count of notified and operational SEZs.

Q10. Answer: (b) Road transport - Bharatmala focuses on expanding and upgrading
