Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (a) Usain Bolt - Bolt set the 9.58 s record at the 2009 World Championships in Berlin.

Q2. Answer: (b) England - England lifted the trophy after a dramatic final against New Zealand at Lord's.

Q3. Answer: (c) Novak Djokovic - As of 2024 Djokovic leads the men's Open Era list with 24 major titles.

Q4. Answer: (d) Budapest - The 2023 World Athletics Championships were held in Budapest, Hungary.

Q5. Answer: (a) Bajrang Punia - Punia secured bronze in the 65 kg freestyle at Tokyo 2020.

Q6. Answer: (b) Belgium - Belgium defeated Australia in the final to claim their first Olympic hockey gold.

Q7. Answer: (c) Muttiah Muralitharan - Muralitharan holds the record with 800 Test wickets.

Q8. Answer: (d) Andre Agassi - Agassi completed the Career Golden Slam by winning all four majors plus Olympic gold.

Q9. Answer: (a) Rudrankksh Patil - Patil won the men's 10m air rifle gold at the 2022 Asian Games in Hangzhou.

Q10. Answer: (b) South Africa - The Springboks beat New Zealand in the final to win the 2023 Rugby World Cup.
