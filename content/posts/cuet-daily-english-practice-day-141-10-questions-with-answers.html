Practice Questions

Q1. Neither the manager nor the employees ____ aware of the new policy.

  1. is
  2. are
  3. was
  4. were

Q2. The scientist's discovery was ____ by the community, leading to further research.

  1. ignored
  2. celebrated
  3. delayed
  4. contested

Q3. Each of the participants have submitted their reports on time.

  1. Each of the participants
  2. have submitted
  3. their reports
  4. on time

Q4. Choose the synonym of “ephemeral”.

  1. lasting
  2. temporary
  3. permanent
  4. eternal

Q5. She is good ____ solving complex puzzles.

  1. at
  2. in
  3. on
  4. with

Q6. Select the sentence with correct punctuation.

  1. "Let's eat, grandma."
  2. "Lets eat grandma."
  3. "Let's eat grandma."
  4. "Lets eat, grandma."

Q7. Choose the correct indirect speech: He said, “I will finish the work tomorrow.”

  1. He said that he would finish the work the next day.
  2. He said that he will finish the work tomorrow.
  3. He said that he would finish the work tomorrow.
  4. He said that he will finish the work the next day.

Q8. Identify the part of speech of the underlined word: “The quickly moving car passed us.”

  1. adjective
  2. adverb
  3. verb
  4. noun

Q9. The committee has ____ its decision.

  1. took
  2. taken
  3. take
  4. taking

Q10. The passage states that the new policy reduces waste by 30%. What does the passage imply about the policy’s effectiveness?

  1. It is ineffective
  2. It moderately reduces waste
  3. It eliminates waste completely
  4. It increases waste

Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (b) are - The verb agrees with the nearest plural subject “employees”.

Q2. Answer: (b) celebrated - The context shows a positive reception leading to further research.

Q3. Answer: (b) have submitted - “Each” is singular, so the verb should be “has submitted”.

Q4. Answer: (b) temporary - “Ephemeral” means lasting a short time; “temporary” is its synonym.

Q5. Answer: (a) at - The adjective “good” is followed by the preposition “at”.

Q6. Answer: (a) “Let's eat, grandma.” - Correct contraction and comma for direct address.

Q7. Answer: (a) He said that he would finish the work the next day. - Backshift tense and change “tomorrow” to “the next day”.

Q8. Answer: (b) adverb - “Quickly” modifies the verb “moving”.

Q9. Answer: (b) taken - Present perfect requires the past participle “taken”.

Q10. Answer: (b) It moderately reduces waste - A 30% reduction indicates a moderate effect.

Common Mistakes

  • Ignoring subject‑verb agreement with compound subjects.
  • Misusing prepositions after adjectives like “good at”.
  • Forgetting to backshift tense in reported speech.

Quick Tips

  • Read the whole sentence before choosing an option.
  • Eliminate clearly wrong choices first.
  • Practice converting direct speech to indirect daily.
  • Review error‑spotting rules for singular/plural subjects.
