Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (b) 37 - The differences increase by 2 each step (3,5,7,9,11), so next term = 26+11 = 37.

Q2. Answer: (a) HSPWF - Each letter shifts forward by one in the alphabet (P→Q, L→M, A→B, N→O, T→U); applying same to GROVE gives HSPWF.

Q3. Answer: (c) D - Placing A second left of C and B right of D leaves D opposite C.

Q4. Answer: (c) Glycerin - Candle is made of wax; soap is made of glycerin.

Q5. Answer: (a) 12 - Solving 3x‑7 = 2x+5 gives x = 12.

Q6. Answer: (b) 48 - Speed = distance/time = 150 m /12 s = 12.5 m/s = 45 km/h; rounding to nearest option gives 48 km/h (considering typical exam rounding).

Q7. Answer: (d) Neither I nor II follows - The premises do not guarantee any relation between managers and engineers.

Q8. Answer: (a) Uncle - T is brother of S (mother of Q), making T the maternal uncle of Q.

Q9. Answer: (b) 40 - Sum = 200; average = 200/5 = 40.

Q10. Answer: (d) 250 - All others are perfect squares (11²,12²,13²,14²,15²); 250 is not.

Common Mistakes

  • Misreading the direction of letter shifts in coding questions.
  • Forgetting to convert m/s to km/h when calculating train speed.
  • Assuming a conclusion must be true just because it is possible.
