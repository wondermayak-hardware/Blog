Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (b) Quick - Rapid means fast, so Quick is the synonym.

Q2. Answer: (b) Plentiful - Abundant means available in large quantity, like Plentiful.

Q3. Answer: (c) Courageous - Brave and Courageous both describe facing danger without fear.

Q4. Answer: (a) Old - Ancient means very old.

Q5. Answer: (d) Hide - Conceal means to keep something out of sight, the same as Hide.

Q6. Answer: (b) Hardworking - A diligent person works carefully and steadily, so Hardworking fits.

Q7. Answer: (c) Delicate - Fragile things break easily, like delicate ones.

Q8. Answer: (a) Huge - Enormous means extremely large, the same as Huge.

Q9. Answer: (c) Unwilling - Reluctant means not wanting to do something, so Unwilling is closest.

Q10. Answer: (b) Authentic - Genuine means real, which is what Authentic means.

Common Mistakes

  • Picking an antonym because it looks familiar.
  • Choosing an option that fits the topic but not the meaning.
  • Skipping unknown words instead of guessing from word parts.

Quick Tips

  • Learn ten new words a day with one example sentence each.
  • Revise missed words the next morning.
  • Read one short English editorial daily to see words in context.
  • Group words by theme, such as emotions or size, to remember them faster.
