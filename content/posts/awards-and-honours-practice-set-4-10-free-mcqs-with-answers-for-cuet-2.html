Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (b) Padma Bhushan - The Padma Bhushan is the second highest Padma award instituted in 1954.

Q2. Answer: (a) Nobel Prize - The Ramon Magsaysay Award is widely called Asia's Nobel Prize.

Q3. Answer: (a) Dr. Raghunath Mashelkar - He was honoured with the UNESCO‑Kalinga Prize in 2022 for science popularisation.

Q4. Answer: (b) Any of the 22 scheduled languages - Sahitya Akademi recognises literary merit across all scheduled languages.

Q5. Answer: (a) Nobel Prize - The Nobel Prize added the Economic Sciences category in 1968.

Q6. Answer: (a) Cinema - Dadasaheb Phalke Award is the highest honour in Indian cinema.

Q7. Answer: (a) 1995 - The International Gandhi Peace Prize was launched by the Government of India in 1995.

Q8. Answer: (a) Viswanathan Anand - Chess grandmaster Anand received the Padma Vibhushan in 2007, the first sportsperson to do so.

Q9. Answer: (a) Literacy - The King Sejong Prize rewards outstanding contributions to literacy worldwide.

Q10. Answer: (c) Sports - The Arjuna Award honours exceptional achievement in sports.

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing the hierarchy of Padma awards (Vibhushan > Bhushan > Shri).
  • Assuming the Ramon Magsaysay Award is a national Indian award.
  • Mixing up the years of institution for the Gandhi Peace Prize and the International Gandhi Peace Prize.
