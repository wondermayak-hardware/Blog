Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (a) Chester Carlson – 1938 - Carlson invented xerography and received the patent in 1938.

Q2. Answer: (b) Carlo Perrier and Emilio Segrè - They produced technetium by bombarding molybdenum with deuterons in 1937.

Q3. Answer: (c) Modicon (by Bedford Associates) - The Modicon 084, the first PLC, was released in 1969.

Q4. Answer: (d) Raytheon - Raytheon manufactured the Radarange, the first commercial microwave oven, in 1947.

Q5. Answer: (a) Steven Sasson - Sasson built the first handheld digital camera prototype at Kodak in 1975.

Q6. Answer: (b) Nick Holonyak Jr. - Holonyak demonstrated the first visible‑red LED while at General Electric in 1962.

Q7. Answer: (c) Leo Baekeland - Baekeland patented Bakelite, the first fully synthetic thermosetting plastic, in 1907.

Q8. Answer: (d) Christiaan Barnard - Barnard performed the world’s first human‑to‑human heart transplant in Cape Town, 1967.

Q9. Answer: (a) Konrad Zuse - Zuse completed the electromechanical Z3, the first programmable computer, in 1941.

Q10. Answer: (b) 1962 - Telstar 1, the first active communications satellite for TV, launched on 10 July 1962.

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing the inventor of the photocopier with later commercial developers.
  • Mixing up the year of the first microwave oven with the later home‑model release.
  • Attributing the first PLC to a major automation brand instead of the original startup.
