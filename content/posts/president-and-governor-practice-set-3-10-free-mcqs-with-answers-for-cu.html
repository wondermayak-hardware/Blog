Quick Answer: This practice set provides ten CUET‑level multiple‑choice questions on the President and Governor, with answer keys and concise explanations to sharpen your constitutional law preparation. It covers ordinance powers, pardoning authority, emergency provisions, and removal procedures.

This set of President and Governor MCQs helps CUET aspirants test their understanding of constitutional powers. Practicing these questions builds confidence for the exam.

How to Use This Set

Practice Questions

Q1. Which article empowers the President to promulgate ordinances when Parliament is not in session?

  1. Article 123
  2. Article 124
  3. Article 122
  4. Article 121

Q2. The Governor of a state can reserve a bill for the consideration of the President under which article?

  1. Article 200
  2. Article 201
  3. Article 199
  4. Article 198

Q3. The President's pardoning power under Article 72 extends to which of the following?

  1. Only death sentences
  2. All offences against Union law
  3. Only military offences
  4. Only state offences

Q4. The Governor can be removed from office before the expiry of his term by which process?

  1. Impeachment by state legislature
  2. Order of the President on advice of Council of Ministers
  3. Resolution of the Legislative Assembly
  4. Judicial review by High Court

Q5. Which of the following statements about the President's ordinance‑making power is correct?

  1. Ordinances have the same force as an Act of Parliament indefinitely
  2. An ordinance must be approved by Parliament within six weeks of reassembly
  3. The President can issue ordinances on any subject without Cabinet advice
  4. Ordinances can amend the Constitution

Q6. The Governor's discretionary powers are exercised in which of the following situations?

  1. Appointment of Chief Minister when no party has clear majority
  2. Giving assent to all bills passed by legislature
  3. Promulgating ordinances on advice of Council of Ministers
  4. Addressing the legislative assembly at the commencement of each session

Q7. The President can declare a Financial Emergency under which article?

  1. Article 352
  2. Article 356
  3. Article 360
  4. Article 365

Q8. When a state Governor reserves a bill for the President's consideration, the President may:

  1. Give assent, withhold assent, or return the bill for reconsideration
  2. Only give assent
  3. Only withhold assent
  4. Refer the bill to the Supreme Court

Q9. The President's role in the appointment of the Chief Election Commissioner is:

  1. Direct appointment without consultation
  2. Appointment on the advice of the Prime Minister alone
  3. Appointment on the advice of the Council of Ministers headed by the Prime Minister
  4. Appointment after approval by Parliament

Q10. Which of the following is NOT a ground for the removal of a Governor?

  1. Violation of the Constitution
  2. Proven misbehavior
  3. Inability to discharge duties due to infirmity
  4. Loss of confidence of the state legislative assembly

Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (a) Article 123 - Article 123 authorises the President to issue ordinances during Parliament's recess.

Q2. Answer: (a) Article 200 - Article 200 empowers the Governor to reserve a bill for the President's consideration.

Q3. Answer: (b) All offences against Union law - Article 72 grants the President pardoning power over all Union‑law offences.

Q4. Answer: (b) Order of the President on advice of Council of Ministers - The President removes a Governor on the advice of the Union Council of Ministers.

Q5. Answer: (b) An ordinance must be approved by Parliament within six weeks of reassembly - Ordinances lapse if not ratified within six weeks of the next session.

Q6. Answer: (a) Appointment of Chief Minister when no party has clear majority - The Governor uses discretion to invite a leader to form government in a hung assembly.

Q7. Answer: (c) Article 360 - Article 360 provides for proclamation of Financial Emergency by the President.

Q8. Answer: (a) Give assent, withhold assent, or return the bill for reconsideration - The President has the same three options as the Governor for reserved bills.

Q9. Answer: (c) Appointment on the advice of the Council of Ministers headed by the Prime Minister - The President acts on the binding advice of the Council of Ministers.

Q10. Answer: (d) Loss of confidence of the state legislative assembly - A Governor cannot be removed merely because the assembly loses confidence.

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing the President's ordinance power (Article 123) with the Governor's (Article 213).
  • Assuming the Governor can be impeached by the state legislature like the President.
  • Believing the President's pardoning power covers state offences.

Quick Tips

  • Memorise the key articles: 123, 200, 72, 360, 356, 352.
  • Link each power to the constitutional authority that exercises it.
  • Practice distinguishing discretionary from ministerial functions of the Governor.
  • Use elimination: identify the only option that matches the constitutional text.
