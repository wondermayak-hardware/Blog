Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (a) 23.5° N - The Tropic of Cancer lies at 23.5° north of the equator.

Q2. Answer: (b) Antarctic - The Antarctic Desert covers about 14 million km², the largest cold desert.

Q3. Answer: (c) Gulf Stream - The Gulf Stream transports warm Atlantic water toward Europe.

Q4. Answer: (d) Ural Mountains - The Urals are conventionally used as the Europe‑Asia divide.

Q5. Answer: (a) La Paz - La Paz, Bolivia, sits at roughly 3,650 m, the highest administrative capital.

Q6. Answer: (b) France - France’s overseas territories give it 12 time zones, the most worldwide.

Q7. Answer: (c) Caspian Sea - The Caspian Sea’s surface area exceeds 370,000 km², larger than any freshwater lake.

Q8. Answer: (d) Strait of Gibraltar - This narrow strait links the Atlantic Ocean to the Mediterranean Sea between Africa and Europe.

Q9. Answer: (a) Arid (BWk) - The Atacama receives virtually no rainfall, fitting the cold arid classification.

Q10. Answer: (b) Rio Grande - The Rio Grande runs about 3,000 km as the US‑Mexico border.

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing the Tropic of Cancer with the Tropic of Capricorn.
  • Assuming the Sahara is the largest desert overall, ignoring the Antarctic.
  • Mixing up the Gulf Stream with the Labrador Current.

Quick Tips

  • Memorise key latitudes (23.5°, 66.5°) for quick recall.
  • Group deserts by hot vs. cold to avoid size confusion.
  • Link major currents to the continents they affect.
  • Use a mental map for mountain ranges serving as continental boundaries.
