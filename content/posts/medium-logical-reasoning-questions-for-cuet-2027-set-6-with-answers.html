Practice Questions

Q1. All engineers are problem solvers. Some problem solvers are creative. Which conclusion follows?

  1. All engineers are creative
  2. Some engineers are creative
  3. No engineer is creative
  4. Cannot be determined

Q2. Find the next number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

  1. 42
  2. 40
  3. 44
  4. 38

Q3. If “CAT” is coded as “DBU” (each letter shifted forward by one), how is “DOG” coded?

  1. EPG
  2. FPI
  3. EPH
  4. DNH

Q4. A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the sister of C. How is D related to B?

  1. Aunt
  2. Mother
  3. Grandmother
  4. Sister

Q5. Starting facing north, you turn right, walk 10 m, turn left, walk 5 m, turn right, walk 10 m. Which direction are you now facing?

  1. North
  2. East
  3. South
  4. West

Q6. Book : Author :: Painting : ?

  1. Canvas
  2. Artist
  3. Gallery
  4. Brush

Q7. All roses are flowers. Some flowers fade quickly. Which statement must be true?

  1. Some roses fade quickly
  2. No rose fades quickly
  3. All flowers are roses
  4. Cannot be determined

Q8. Five friends P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row facing north. P sits at the left end. Q sits immediately right of P. R sits between Q and S. T sits at the right end. Who sits in the middle?

  1. P
  2. Q
  3. R
  4. S

Q9. Complete the letter series: A, C, E, G, ?

  1. I
  2. H
  3. J
  4. K

Q10. Statement: “If the library extends its hours, more students will use it.” Assumptions: I. Students currently cannot use the library due to limited hours. II. Extending hours will not increase operating costs. Which assumption is implicit?

  1. Only I is implicit
  2. Only II is implicit
  3. Both are implicit
  4. Neither is implicit

Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (b) Some engineers are creative - From “some problem solvers are creative” and all engineers are problem solvers, at least some engineers may be creative.

Q2. Answer: (a) 42 - The series follows n×(n+1): 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42.

Q3. Answer: (c) EPH - Each letter of DOG shifted forward by one gives E, P, H.

Q4. Answer: (a) Aunt - D is sister of C (father of B), making D the aunt of B.

Q5. Answer: (b) East - Right turn from north faces east, left turn faces north, right turn faces east again.

Q6. Answer: (b) Artist - An author creates a book; an artist creates a painting.

Q7. Answer: (d) Cannot be determined - “Some flowers fade quickly” does not guarantee any rose does.

Q8. Answer: (c) R - Seating order: P‑Q‑R‑S‑T; middle position is R.

Q9. Answer: (a) I - Series progresses by skipping one alphabet letter each step.

Q10. Answer: (a) Only I is implicit - The statement assumes limited hours restrict usage; cost impact is not assumed.

Common Mistakes

  • Assuming “some” implies “all” in syllogisms
  • Ignoring the direction of turns in spatial reasoning
  • Choosing the first plausible option without checking all choices

Quick Tips

  • Draw a quick diagram for seating or direction problems
  • Identify the logical structure (syllogism, series, coding) before solving
  • Eliminate obviously wrong options to increase guess accuracy
  • Practice timed sets to build speed and confidence
