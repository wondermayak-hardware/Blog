Practice Questions

Q1. If the letters of the word "PLANET" are rearranged alphabetically, which letter will be third from the left?

  1. A
  2. E
  3. L
  4. N

Q2. In a certain code, "MANGO" is written as "NZOHL". How will "APPLE" be written in the same code?

  1. BQQMF
  2. ZOKKD
  3. BQQMF
  4. ZOKKD

Q3. A, B, C, D, and E are sitting in a row facing north. B sits immediately to the right of A. C sits at one of the ends. D sits between B and E. Who sits at the extreme left?

  1. A
  2. C
  3. E
  4. D

Q4. Find the missing number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, ?, 42

  1. 28
  2. 30
  3. 32
  4. 34

Q5. Statement: All poets are dreamers. Some dreamers are writers. Conclusions: I. Some poets are writers. II. No poet is a writer.

  1. Only I follows
  2. Only II follows
  3. Either I or II follows
  4. Neither I nor II follows

Q6. If "ROSE" is coded as "TLUG", what is the code for "LILY"?

  1. NKNA
  2. NKMA
  3. NKOA
  4. NKPA

Q7. Pointing to a photograph, a woman says, "He is the only son of my mother's only daughter." How is the man related to the woman?

  1. Brother
  2. Son
  3. Nephew
  4. Husband

Q8. Which of the following figures completes the pattern? (Assume a 2x2 grid where the top‑left has a circle, top‑right a triangle, bottom‑left a square, bottom‑right ?)

  1. Circle
  2. Triangle
  3. Square
  4. Pentagon

Q9. A train travels 60 km in 45 minutes. What is its average speed in km/h?

  1. 70
  2. 80
  3. 90
  4. 100

Q10. Choose the word that best fits the analogy: "Candle : Wax :: Battery : ?"

  1. Energy
  2. Acid
  3. Electrode
  4. Power

Answer Key with Explanations

Q1. Answer: (c) L - Alphabetical order of PLANET gives A, E, L, N, P, T; third letter is L.

Q2. Answer: (a) BQQMF - Each letter shifted +1 forward in alphabet (M→N, A→B, N→O, G→H, O→P) gives BQQMF.

Q3. Answer: (b) C - With C at an end and arrangement A‑B‑D‑E, C occupies extreme left.

Q4. Answer: (b) 30 - Differences increase by 2: +4, +6, +8, +10 → 20+10=30.

Q5. Answer: (d) Neither I nor II follows - No definite link between poets and writers can be drawn.

Q6. Answer: (a) NKNA - Pattern: each letter +2, +2, +2, +2 (R→T, O→Q, S→U, E→G) applied to LILY gives NKNA.

Q7. Answer: (b) Son - Woman's mother's only daughter is the woman herself; her only son is her son.

Q8. Answer: (d) Pentagon - Shapes progress by adding one side each step: circle(0), triangle(3), square(4), pentagon(5).

Q9. Answer: (b) 80 - 60 km in 0.75 h → 60/0.75 = 80 km/h.

Q10. Answer: (a) Energy - Candle consumes wax to produce light; battery stores energy to supply power.
